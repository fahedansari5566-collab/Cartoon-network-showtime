Adventure Time is an American animated television series created by Pendleton Ward. The show follows the adventures of Finn, a boy, and his best friend Jake, a dog with magical powers, in the Land of Ooo.

ABOUT THE SHOW

Adventure Time is set in the post-apocalyptic Land of Ooo. It follows the adventures of Finn the Human and Jake the Dog as they explore, help others, and face powerful enemies. The series is full of fun, friendship, and life lessons.
